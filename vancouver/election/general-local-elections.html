Vancouver at a glance

What the evidence suggests

Our assessment: Sim has a past citywide victory and led Research Co.'s decided sample. Fry led the Pallas snapshot; Allam was competitive in both. Hardwick and Bligh also registered support. These are reasons to watch those campaigns, not grounds to declare a winner.

Candidate departures may change the race, but their supporters cannot simply be added to another candidate's total. Fresh polling using the current candidate list would provide a stronger basis for assessing winning chances. Neither these mayoral polls nor the two-seat 2025 result supports individual council, Park Board or school-trustee win probabilities.
