Vancouver provincial election 2026

Saturday, October 24 · 8 am–8 pm Pacific time

Voting essentials

Advance voting
October 16–21 · 8 am–8 pm.Opening days vary by location.
Your voting place
Check your Where to Vote card for your riding and polling place. Need help? Elections BC: 1-800-661-8683.
What to bring
For example, a BC driver’s licence; or two documents showing your name, with at least one showing your current address.

Who can vote: Canadian citizens aged 18+ on October 24 who have lived in BC since April 23, 2026.

Your riding’s candidates

One MLA per riding. Party labels are affiliations printed on the ballot.
