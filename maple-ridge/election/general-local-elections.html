01 / The calendar

Dates that shape the election

Maple Ridge's general local election is on October 17, 2026. All times below are local to Maple Ridge.

  1. September 1–11

    Candidate nominations

    Nominations opened September 1 at 9 am and closed September 11 at 4 pm.

  2. September 18

    Withdrawal deadline

    The deadline for candidates to withdraw was 4 pm.

  3. September 19

    Campaign period begins

    The regulated campaign period began at 12:01 am.

  4. September 21

    Election declaration

    The City's declaration lists the candidates shown on this page.

  5. October 7, 10, 14 & 15

    Advance election dates

    Four advance dates are scheduled before the general election.

  6. October 17

    General election day

    Saturday, 8 am–8 pm. The election covers mayor, council and Maple Ridge's School District 42 trustee seats.

04 / Reading the race

Who has the best chance of winning?

Assessment · September 23

No reliable numerical odds yet

We did not locate a reliable public head-to-head voting-intention poll for Kok versus Ruimy in the material reviewed. There is not enough evidence here to name a statistically supported favourite or assign winning percentages.

Dan Ruimy's position

Ruimy has the record and visibility of an incumbent, plus a demonstrated citywide victory in 2022. That makes his record a central part of the contest. It does not show how many of those voters will support him again.

Jerry Kok's position

Kok brings a business-community and advisory-committee background. In this two-candidate race, he is the alternative to the incumbent. Without current polling, the size of his support is unmeasured here.

What could change the assessment?

A transparent local poll with fieldwork dates, sample size and methodology would be more useful than online popularity surveys or campaign claims. Turnout and voters' assessment of the candidates' records and proposals will matter; previous totals cannot simply be transferred to 2026.

There is also insufficient candidate-level polling here to estimate individual council or school-trustee winning chances. These are separate contests for multiple seats.

This is an independent editorial assessment, not an official forecast. Candidate information and historical results are presented as dated facts; no live results are claimed.
